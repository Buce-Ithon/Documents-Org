* Email Writing: Invitation to the Graduation Ceremony
  :PROPERTIES:
  :TYPE:     Formal Email
  :TOPIC:    Invitation
  :STATUS:   Learning
  :END:

** Writing Task

Write an email to all international experts on campus, inviting them to attend the graduation ceremony. Include the time, place, and other relevant information.

** Model Answer

Dear International Experts,

On behalf of the university, I am delighted to invite you to attend our graduation ceremony. The ceremony will be held at 9:00 a.m. on June 20th in the University Auditorium. It will be a memorable occasion as we celebrate the achievements of our graduating students. The ceremony will include speeches by university leaders, student representatives, and a presentation of graduation certificates. Your presence would mean a great deal to our graduates and make the occasion even more special. We sincerely hope you can join us. If it is convenient for you, we would be grateful if you could arrive about 15 minutes early.

Best regards,
Li Ming

** Useful Expressions

*** Opening

**** On behalf of the university, I am delighted to invite you to ...
**** I am writing to invite you to attend ...
**** It is my great pleasure to invite you to ...

*** Time and Place

**** The ceremony will be held at 9:00 a.m. on June 20th.
**** The event will take place in the University Auditorium.
**** Please arrive about 15 minutes early.

*** Event Information

**** We will celebrate the achievements of our graduating students.
**** The ceremony will include speeches by university leaders and student representatives.
**** There will also be a presentation of graduation certificates.

*** Expressing Importance

**** Your presence would mean a great deal to our graduates.
**** Your attendance would make the occasion even more special.
**** We sincerely hope you can join us.

*** Closing

**** We look forward to seeing you at the ceremony.
**** We would be grateful if you could attend.
**** Best regards,

** Writing Structure

1. Greeting
2. State the purpose of the email
3. Provide the time and place
4. Introduce the main activities
5. Explain why the recipient's attendance is important
6. Express hope and provide practical information
7. Sign off with "Li Ming"

* Essay Writing: An Optimistic Attitude Toward Life
  :PROPERTIES:
  :TYPE:     Cartoon Essay
  :TOPIC:    Optimism and Attitude
  :STATUS:   Learning
  :END:

** Writing Task

Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the cartoon. In your essay, you should:

1. describe the drawing briefly;
2. explain its intended meaning;
3. give your comments.

** Model Answer

The cartoon presents two men standing beside a bottle that has fallen to the ground, with much of its contents spilled out. The man on the left looks upset and holds his head, saying, "Everything is gonel!" By contrast, the man on the right smiles and says, "Fortunately, there is still some left."

Simple as the picture is, its message is profound. Faced with exactly the same situation, people may have completely different attitudes. A pessimistic person tends to focus on loss and difficulty, while an optimistic person is more likely to notice what remains and look for possible solutions. Therefore, our attitude can greatly influence how we feel and what we do in difficult situations.

In my view, difficulties and setbacks are unavoidable in life. Instead of complaining about what we have lost, we should remain positive and make the best use of what is still available. Only by keeping hope and taking action can we turn difficulties into opportunities and move toward success.

* Essay Writing: Changes in the Market Shares of Passenger Car Brands
  :PROPERTIES:
  :TYPE:     Chart Essay
  :TOPIC:    Market Share and Domestic Brands
  :STATUS:   Learning
  :END:

** Writing Task

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your essay, you should:

1. interpret the chart;
2. give your comments.

You should write at least 150 words.

** Model Answer

The chart illustrates the changes in the market shares of three types of passenger car brands in China in 2008 and 2009. As is clearly shown, domestic brands accounted for 25% of the market in 2008, but their share rose to about 32% in 2009. In contrast, Japanese brands experienced a decline from 35% to 25%. Meanwhile, the market share of American brands remained unchanged at 15%.

Several factors may account for these changes. First, domestic automobile manufacturers have made considerable progress in product quality, technology and design, making their cars increasingly attractive to Chinese consumers. Second, domestic brands are usually more competitive in terms of price and after-sales service. In addition, the growing sense of national pride among consumers may also have contributed to their increasing popularity. The decline of Japanese brands may be partly related to intensified market competition.

In my opinion, the growing market share of domestic brands is an encouraging sign. Nevertheless, Chinese automobile manufacturers should continue to improve their technology, quality and innovation. Only by enhancing their competitiveness can they win the trust of consumers and achieve long-term development.

*** Chart Description

**** As is clearly shown, ...
**** ... accounted for ... of the market.
**** Its share rose from ... to ...
**** ... experienced a decline from ... to ...
**** The market share of ... remained unchanged at ...

*** Explaining Changes

**** ... have made considerable progress in ...

**** ... are more competitive in terms of ...
**** ... may also have contributed to ...
**** ... may be partly related to ...

*** Giving Comments

**** In my opinion, ...
**** ... is an encouraging sign.

**** achieve long-term development

** Writing Structure

1. Introduce the chart and identify the subject.
2. Describe the major changes in the data.
3. Highlight the contrast between different categories.
4. Explain the possible reasons behind the changes.
5. Give your personal comments.
6. Offer suggestions for future development.
7. Conclude with the significance of improving competitiveness.
