* wobble

"Wobble" means *to move or shake unsteadily from side to side*, such as when a stable has uneven legs, a person rides a bicycle poorly, or a voice [[tremble][trembles]] with emotion.

* tremble

"Tremble" means *to shake involuntarily, typically as a result of anxiety, excitement, weakness, or cold*.

* grill

"Grill" primarily means *to cook food on a metal frame over direct heat*, but it can also mean *to question someone intensely*.

* grate

"Grate" means *to [[rub][rub]] food against a sharp, [[rough][rough]] tool to [[shred][shred]] it*, or a framework or metal bars used to cover an opening.

* rub

"Rub" means *to press and move your hand or an object back and forth against a surface*.

* rough

"Rough" means *having an uneven surface that is not smooth, or something that is harsh, violent, or approximate*.

* shred

"Shred" means *to cut, tear, or break something into narrow, thin strips*.

* coarse

"Coarse" means *having a rough texture, consisting of large particles, or lacking [[refine][refinement]] in manners or language*.

* refine

"Refine" means *to make something pure, precise, or better by removing unwanted parts*, while refinement is the process or the result of making those improvements.

* fervor

"Fervor" means *an intense, passionate, or serious feeling about something*.

* benediction

"Benediction" means *a prayer asking for God's blessing, usually spoken at the end of a religious service*.

* agoraphobia

"Agoraphobia" is *an anxiety disorder where a person fears and avoids places or situations that might cause panic, feeling trapped, helpless, or embarrassed*.

* discrepancy

A "discrepancy" is *a difference or inconsistency between two things that should be same*, such as numbers, facts, or stories.

* bespoke

"Bespoke" means *made specially for a particular customer*.

* devise

To "devise" means to *invent, plan, or create a strayegy, system, or object by using your mind and imagination*. In legal terms, it can also mean to leave property to someone through a will.

* entail

"Entail" means *to involve, require, or make something necessary as a logical consequence or unavoidable result*. For example, a new job might entail long hours, or a big project entails hard work.

* pivotal

"Pivotal" means *having central, crucial importance because other things depend on it*. It comes from the word pivot, meaning a pin or central point that something turns around. When an event or person is pivotal, a whole situation turns or hinges on them.

* poke

The word "poke" most commonly means *to push or jab quickly with a finger or a sharp object*. However, it has several different meanings depending on whether it is used as a physical action, social media feature, slang, or to describe a popular Hawaiian food dish.

** Physical Actions and Movements
*** Jab or prod
To push hard or fast with a finger, stick, or elbow (e.g., /to poke someone in the ribs/).
*** Stick out
To cause a part of something to peek or protrude out from a space (e.g., /poking your head out a window/).
*** Rummage
To search around inside a small or tight space aimlessly (e.g., /poking around in a drawer/).
*** Move slowly
To walk or travel lazily without a clear goal.

** Digital and Slang Meanings
*** Social media
On platforms like Facebook, a "poke" is a light virtual feature used to wave, say hello, or get another user's attention.
*** Meddling
To "poke your nose" into something means to be nosy or interfere in other people's business.

** Food (Poké)
*** The Hawaiian dish
Spelled =poké= (pronounced /POH-kay/), it is a traditional dish of diced raw fish - most commonly tuna - marinated in soy sauce, sesame oil, and seasonings.

* stumble

To "stumble" means *to trip, lose your balance while walking, or find something by accident*.

- *Trip* :: To hit your foot on an object and almost fall.
