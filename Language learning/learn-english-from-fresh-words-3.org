* wobble

"Wobble" means *to move or shake unsteadily from side to side*, such as when a stable has uneven legs, a person rides a bicycle poorly, or a voice [[tremble][trembles]] with emotion.

* tremble

"Tremble" means *to shake involuntarily, typically as a result of anxiety, excitement, weakness, or cold*.

* grill

"Grill" primarily means *to cook food on a metal frame over direct heat*, but it can also mean *to question someone intensely*.

* grate

"Grate" means *to [[rub][rub]] food against a sharp, [[rough][rough]] tool to [[shred][shred]] it*, or a framework or metal bars used to cover an opening.

* rub

"Rub" means *to press and move your hand or an object back and forth against a surface*.

* rough

"Rough" means *having an uneven surface that is not smooth, or something that is harsh, violent, or approximate*.

* shred

"Shred" means *to cut, tear, or break something into narrow, thin strips*.

* coarse

"Coarse" means *having a rough texture, consisting of large particles, or lacking [[refine][refinement]] in manners or language*.

* refine

"Refine" means *to make something pure, precise, or better by removing unwanted parts*, while refinement is the process or the result of making those improvements.

* fervor

"Fervor" means *an intense, passionate, or serious feeling about something*.

* benediction

"Benediction" means *a prayer asking for God's blessing, usually spoken at the end of a religious service*.

* agoraphobia

"Agoraphobia" is *an anxiety disorder where a person fears and avoids places or situations that might cause panic, feeling trapped, helpless, or embarrassed*.
